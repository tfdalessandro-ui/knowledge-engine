```

  Trigger: Mon 2026-09-07 03:00:00 UTC

  Main PID: 2357825 (code=exited, status=0/SUCCESS)
  fetched=0 skipped_duplicate=12 skipped_robots_disallowed=0 errors=0
```

**Real finding, disclosed rather than hidden**: `crawl.pipeline` has NO
re-fetch/TTL mechanism -- it dedups by canonical URL forever, so it will
report `fetched=0` on every future run indefinitely unless the allowlist
itself grows. The timer fires and the mechanism runs correctly (which is
what Step 1 asked for); "two runs ~24h apart showing new fetches" will
not happen with this crawler's current design, and I said so before it
could look like a mystery tomorrow.

## Step 6 — KG relation-extraction expansion

Re-ran `kg.pipeline` on the now-47-doc corpus (was 40 at the original
baseline):
```
documents_processed=47 entities_extracted=908 relations_extracted=9 merge_candidates_queued=3
Live graph: entity_count=215, entity_to_entity_relations=9
```
**Real finding: entities grew 3.5x (62->215) but entity-to-entity
relations stayed at EXACTLY 9 -- unchanged.** All 9 trace back to the
same two original hand-written clean-SVO documents
(`doc34_kg_relations_demo`, `doc35_kg_more_technologies`); zero relations
came from any of the newer prose-style content (7 crawled Wikipedia
pages, other corpus growth). **Assessment: this is NOT "expected at
current scale"** -- three effective doublings of entity count with zero
relation growth rules out "just needs more data," and points at the
rule-based word-order extractor only firing on short clean-SVO sentences,
not natural prose. Recommendation: needs more/broader patterns or a
different approach for prose text. Not fixed in this entry (Step 6 asked
for assessment, not a rewrite).

### New code (permanent, not ad-hoc)

- `src/research/pipeline.py` -- thin wrapper reusing
  `crawl.pipeline.run_crawl()`'s exact mechanics (no duplicated crawl
  logic) against the research allowlist, its own output dir/state db,
  and arXiv's real 15s crawl-delay (vs P6's default 3.0s).
- `src/research/candidates.py` -- `CandidateStore` (SQLite): register a
  candidate (name, source, description, config_diff JSON,
  benchmark_before/after, auto-computed 14-day decision_date), query
  pending-past-decision-date, set status, record/read a rolling version
  score history.
- `scripts/research_loop.py` -- the daily driver: `decide()` (pure
  function, the mechanical promote/drop rule) and `check_rollback()`
  (trailing-5-average comparison), plus the batch runner that applies
  both and writes a LOGBOOK entry only when something actually happened
  (skip-and-one-line-log otherwise, same discipline as
  `scripts/auto_tune.py`).
- `config/__init__.py` -- 4 new Settings fields
  (`research_crawl_output_dir`, `research_crawl_state_db_path`,
  `research_crawl_politeness_budget_s`, `research_candidates_db_path`).
