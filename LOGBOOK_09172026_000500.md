## A mistake I made, and the fix

To fix the remaining text references (activate scripts, `pyvenv.cfg`), I ran a text search over the venv that also matched
**5,260 compiled `.pyc` cache files** (they embed their source path), and rewrote them as UTF-8 text. That corrupted their
binary contents. I checked before fixing: the damage was only `.pyc` caches. No `.pyd`, `.dll` or other binary was touched,
and the other 10 matches were real text scripts, which were rewritten correctly. Fix: a guarded script deleted exactly those 5,260
files (it would have refused any path that wasn't a `.pyc` inside the venv's `__pycache__` folders, or that had no `.py` source)
and rebuilt them from the untouched sources: 5,152 rebuilt immediately, the rest are regenerated by Python on first import.
